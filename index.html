Why Choose Camp Horizon?

We build confidence, teamwork, and outdoor skills in a safe and supportive setting.

🌲 Outdoor Adventure

Guided mountain trekking, kayaking, campfire survival skills, and wilderness exploration for all experience levels.

🛡️ Certified Safety

Strict 1:6 counselor-to-camper ratio with a 24/7 medical station and certified lifeguards on site.

🎨 Creative Workshops

Hands-on art, music, theater, and digital media tracks that nurture creativity alongside physical activities.

🤝 Lifelong Friendships

Team athletic games, cabin bonding, and evening traditions designed to build lasting bonds and confidence.
