Our Journey & History

How Camp Horizon grew into a modern youth development center.

The Foundation Years (2012–2015)

June 2012: Camp Horizon formally opens its gates with a single wooden lodge, focusing on foundational wilderness skills such as cozy campfires, stargazing under clear night skies, forest camping, bushcraft, and guided mountain treks.

August 2014: Recognizing the attraction of the lake, the camp expands its outdoor repertoire to include water safety courses and kayak excursions.

Expansion & Specialization (2016–2020)

As attendance grew, Camp Horizon evolved from a traditional wilderness retreat into a multifaceted youth development center, introducing structured, specialized tracks to cater to diverse passions:

  • Spring 2016: The Adventure Camp (ages 7–17) and Sports Camp (ages 7–17) are officially established, adding competitive athletic fields, track and field tournaments, and team navigation challenges.
  • Summer 2017: Recognizing the creative spark in young campers, the Creative Camp (ages 7–17) launches alongside a dedicated Creative Arts & Crafting Hub.
  • Fall 2019: The Music Camp (ages 8–16) is introduced, offering vocal training, instrument lessons, and live performance showcases.

Modern Innovation & Modernization (2021–Present)

Summer 2021: Blending traditional outdoor life with modern skills, Camp Horizon introduces the IT Camp (ages 10–17) and Science Camp (ages 9–16), offering workshops in programming, web development, technology, and interactive scientific experimentation.

Present Day: Today, Camp Horizon stands as a vibrant hub where classic outdoor adventure meets modern creativity and technological exploration, bringing together thousands of campers each year to build lifelong friendships.
