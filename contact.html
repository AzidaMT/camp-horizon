Frequently Asked Questions

What is the counselor-to-camper ratio?

We maintain a strict 1:6 ratio for all age brackets to ensure maximum safety and attention during every outdoor activity.

What medical facilities are available on-site?

Our camp has a 24/7 medical station staffed with a certified pediatrician and a fully equipped first-aid tent.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Full refunds are available up to 14 days before the start of the booked session. Within 14 days, bookings can be transferred to another date at no penalty.
